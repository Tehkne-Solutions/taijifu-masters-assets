## Identity conclusion

The v1.0.0 pack contains the correct Lian Wu identity. It is not necessary to redesign the character from zero.

Stable identity traits recovered across the master and turnaround:

- juvenile/boyish martial-fighter read;
- large-head comic/manga fighter proportions;
- black high topknot;
- blue hair tie/ribbons;
- white martial coat with water-blue diagonal construction;
- asymmetric dark shoulder treatment with gold ornament;
- broad blue front sash;
- black trousers;
- black/gold forearm and shin guards;
- blue single-katana sheath.

## Defects blocking Character Lock

`source/char_lian_wu__master_raw.png`

### Front

`turnaround/char_lian_wu__front_raw.png`

Identity and costume are consistent, but the left hand/weapon area contains multiple narrow gray blade-like shapes. Neutral turnaround must read as exactly one sheathed katana and no loose/drawn blade.

### Side left

`turnaround/char_lian_wu__side_left_raw.png`

Identity and silhouette remain consistent. The hand/weapon area contains stray narrow blade-like geometry. Preserve one blue sheath only and remove any second-weapon read.

### Back

`turnaround/char_lian_wu__back_raw.png`

This is the cleanest continuity anchor for coat, sash, hair ribbons and the single blue sheath. Use this view to resolve weapon placement in the corrected set.

### Side right

`turnaround/char_lian_wu__side_right_raw.png`

This is the strongest blocker. It visibly contains a drawn sword plus additional blade-like forms while the blue sheath remains visible. Neutral turnaround must show one katana total, sheathed, with empty hands.
